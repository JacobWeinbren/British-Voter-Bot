British Voter Bot

Real British voters, one at a time.

Every card on this account is one real, anonymous respondent to the British Election Study, Britain's largest survey of voters. Each card says who they are, what they think, and how they'd vote, in their own answers to questions written by the BES. The views are the respondent's own, not ours.

How to read a card

{{ map_svg | safe }}
The dot is where they live. Each voter is drawn over their home nation, marked at their constituency.
The bubbles are their views. Favourite and least favourite leader, the issues they care about, how they see their nation - each one the respondent's own answer to a BES question, not the opinion of this account, its authors or the BES.
The scales show where they sit. Two BES 0-10 scales: economic left-right, social liberal-authoritarian. The tick is the centre of the scale (5); the lilac band is the voters' interquartile range.
2024 → today
The band is their vote. How they voted in 2024, who they'd vote for now - coloured by the party they're backing today, grey if they're undecided or wouldn't vote.
