British Voter Bot
Real British voters, one at a time.
Every card on this account is one real, anonymous respondent to the British Election Study, Britain’s largest survey of voters. Each card says who they are, what they think, and how they’d vote, in their own survey answers.
How to read a card
The dot is where they live. Each voter is drawn over their home nation, marked at their constituency.
The bubbles are their views. Favourite and least favourite leader, the issues they care about, how they see their nation - all from their survey answers.
The scales show where they sit. BES 0-10 value scales: economic left-right and liberal-authoritarian. The tick marks the scale midpoint (5 on the 0-10 scale); the darker band is the interquartile range of voters (25th-75th percentile).
2024 → today
The band is their vote. How they voted in 2024, who they’d vote for now - coloured by the party they’re backing today.
Together, they are Britain in miniature. Voters are drawn from the wave’s 31,392 respondents in proportion to BES survey weights, so the feed reflects the British adult population by age, gender, region, past vote and more. BES Wave 31 · YouGov · May-June 2026
No one is the average voter.
Three voters a day
Taken one at a time, voters are messier - and more interesting - than any stereotype.
